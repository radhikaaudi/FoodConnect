title: Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Food Donation Act (1996)
source: 42 U.S.C. § 1791 — federal donor liability protection
---
The Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Food Donation Act protects food donors — businesses,
nonprofits, and gleaners — from civil and criminal liability when they donate apparently
wholesome food in good faith to a nonprofit for distribution to needy individuals.
Protection does not apply in cases of gross negligence or intentional misconduct. The 2023
Food Donation Improvement Act extended protection to direct donations and qualified
direct-to-consumer donors. Practical takeaway: a donor acting in good faith, donating food
that meets safety standards at the time of donation, is federally shielded from liability.
