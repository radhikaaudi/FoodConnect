id: usda-danger-zone
title: USDA Temperature Danger Zone (40°F–140°F)
source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service — "Danger Zone" guidance
---
Bacteria grow most rapidly between 40°F and 140°F (the "Danger Zone"), doubling in as
little as 20 minutes. Perishable food (meat, poultry, protein, seafood, dairy, cooked food,
cut produce) left unrefrigerated in the Danger Zone for more than 2 hours should be
discarded; above 90°F the limit drops to 1 hour. A common operational threshold used by food banks is a hard 4-hour
cumulative limit for time out of temperature control, with anything past 2 hours treated
as elevated risk requiring judgment.
