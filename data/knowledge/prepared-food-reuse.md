---
id: prepared-food-reuse
title: Donating Prepared & Previously-Served Food
source: FDA Food Code + food-rescue operational practice
---
Prepared food that was cooked but never served (e.g., untouched catering trays, buffet
backup pans held at temperature) is generally safe to donate if it was held per the Food
Code and cooled/handled correctly. Food that has been plated, served, or sat on a consumer
buffet should not be redistributed. Hot food should be cooled from 135°F to 70°F within 2
hours and to 41°F within 6 total hours before donation. Practical takeaway: untouched,
temperature-held catering is donatable; leftovers from individual plates are not.
