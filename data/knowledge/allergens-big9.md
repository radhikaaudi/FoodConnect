id: allergens-big9
title: The Big-9 Major Food Allergens (FASTER Act)
source: FDA — FALCPA + FASTER Act (2021)
---
US law recognizes nine major food allergens that must be declared: milk, eggs, fish,
crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, and sesame (added by the FASTER
Act, effective 2023). When redistributing donated food, allergen information must travel
with the food. Prepared/unlabeled food with unknown allergens should not be routed to
recipients serving populations with declared dietary restrictions; when labels are missing,
treat allergen status as unknown and prefer recipients without restrictions.
