id: tax-deduction
title: Enhanced Tax Deduction for Food Donations
source: Internal Revenue Code § 170(e)(3)
---
Businesses donating wholesome food inventory to qualified nonprofits may claim an enhanced
deduction: generally the lesser of (a) twice the basis of the donated food, or (b) basis
plus half the expected profit margin. The enhanced deduction is available to C-corps and,
since 2015 (PATH Act), to other business entities, with caps tied to income. Practical
takeaway: a donation receipt recording item, quantity, date, and recipient nonprofit
supports the donor's deduction claim — turning surplus into a financial benefit rather than
a disposal cost.
