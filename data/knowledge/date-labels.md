title: Date Labels — Best By vs Use By, and Donatability
source: USDA/FDA date-labeling guidance
---
Most date labels ("Best if Used By", "Sell By") indicate peak quality, not safety — food
past these dates is generally still safe and donatable if it was stored properly and shows
no spoilage. "Use By" on infant formula is the main safety-critical date and must be
honored. Shelf-stable goods (canned, dry) with minor packaging damage (small dents, not on
seams, no swelling or rust) remain donatable. Practical takeaway: past "best by" ≠ waste;
evaluate storage history and condition rather than the printed date alone.
