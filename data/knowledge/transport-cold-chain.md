id: transport-cold-chain
title: Cold-Chain Transport for Rescued Food
source: Food-bank operational guidance (Feeding America safe-handling standards)
---
Refrigerated or frozen donations must be moved in temperature-controlled or insulated
transport and kept at or below 40°F (frozen: 0°F). Short runs (under ~30 minutes) with
insulated containers and ice packs are generally acceptable for refrigerated items; raw
proteins and dairy should not travel unrefrigerated. On arrival, receiving staff should
verify temperature before accepting. Practical takeaway: never assign perishable loads to
drivers without refrigerated or properly insulated transport.
